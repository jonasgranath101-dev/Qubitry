AI 3D Shape Generator

About

This tool uses OneCoreAI to generate advanced 3D shapes. The AI system analyzes your description and generates appropriate geometric parameters for creating complex shapes like spheres, cylinders, cones, tori, and more.

Supported shapes: cube, sphere, cylinder, cone, torus, octahedron

How it works: Multiple AI cores are trained to determine optimal scale and shape parameters based on your input description.
